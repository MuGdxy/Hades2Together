The bundled native loader is a modified Hell2Modding 1.0.125, including
the Hell2Modding-GLOBAL fallback plugin. Hell2Modding and the loader's
base are MIT licensed. The Hades2Together native overlay provides the
Hades2Together input API.
Hell2Modding source: https://github.com/SGG-Modding/Hell2Modding/tree/a80069739d1333591c7ddfb89b606d9101bd31f4
Overlay source: https://github.com/MuGdxy/Hades2Together/tree/h2t-development/native/host-input

Tolk (accessibility and screen-reader support) is statically linked.
Tolk is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3.
The loader includes Tolk's NVDA interface with its LGPL 2.1 notice.
Tolk source: https://github.com/xiaoxiao921/tolk/tree/91e9aec

installer. It contains the matching source and relinking instructions.
