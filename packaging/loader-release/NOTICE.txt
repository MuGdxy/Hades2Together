Hades2Together Windows preview loader

This loader includes Tolk by Davy Kager and contributors, statically linked
under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3. For Tolk source,
license texts and the recipe to use a modified version, see the accompanying
Hades2Together-Loader-Source.zip distributed alongside this release. The
license directory includes GNU LGPLv3, GNU GPLv3 and the upstream LGPLv2.1
notice for the optional NVDA controller client. Tolk may use external
screen reader clients when separately present on the system.

The loader incorporates MIT-licensed Hell2Modding by SGG-Modding contributors
and Hades2Together party preview changes. The source companion identifies the
upstream commit and source differences in provenance.json and README.md.
Third-party license texts are in the licenses directory.

The native build uses ReturnOfModdingBase, AsyncLogger, Dear ImGui with its
stb components and Tristan Grimmer's ProggyClean font, Sol2, the Hades II Lua
fork, LPeg, LuaSocket, EASTL and EABase, raw_pdb, Capstone, SafetyHook,
PolyHook 2, AsmJit, AsmTK, Zydis and Zycore, pugixml, nlohmann/json,
unordered_dense, cpp-semver, toml++, magic_enum, and lovely-lib. The source
companion records their exact revisions and the Rust dependency lock and
notices. Corrosion is used as a build tool.
